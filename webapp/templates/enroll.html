Built in: the Passwords app. Nothing to install.

  1. Make sure this login is saved in Passwords. When you signed up, your iPhone should have offered to save it -- if you tapped Not Now, add it in the Passwords app first.
  2. Open the Camera app and point it at the QR code. Tap the banner that appears to set up a verification code.

Prefer a separate app? Open Google Authenticator, tap +, choose Scan a QR code, and scan from inside the app.
